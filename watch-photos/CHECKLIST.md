# Photo drop-off checklist

Find the watch in the visual guide at **http://localhost:3000/photo-guide**, then
drop its photos into the folder listed below. No renaming needed.

Inside a folder, you can optionally name files `front.jpg`, `angled.jpg`,
`caseback.jpg`, `wrist.jpg`, or `detail.jpg`. Otherwise I will inspect and assign.

If you can't identify a watch, put the photo in that brand's `_unsorted` folder
and tell me — I'll match it for you.
